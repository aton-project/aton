# AX-0001 — AX Charter

## Purpose

AX (ATON Experience) defines how engineering knowledge is presented,
explored and understood.

AX is not a UI framework.

AX is not a CSS theme.

AX is the specification of the user experience for every ATON application.

---

## Mission

Enable engineers to understand complex engineering knowledge quickly,
confidently and consistently.

---

## Vision

Engineering knowledge shall be:

- easy to discover
- easy to navigate
- easy to understand
- easy to trust
- easy to evolve

---

## Core Principle

> Every View Serves Understanding.

Every interaction shall improve understanding.

---

## Goals

- Reduce cognitive load.
- Reveal context.
- Expose relationships.
- Encourage exploration.
- Remain predictable.
- Scale to large engineering projects.

---

## Non Goals

AX does not define:

- engineering content
- storage
- rendering logic
- business rules

These belong to other ATON layers.
