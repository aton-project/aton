# governs

## Meaning

An artifact establishes rules, principles, or constraints governing another
artifact or artifact collection.

## Direction

The Predicate is directional.

The source establishes governance over the target.

## Allowed Pairs

The Predicate is valid only for the explicitly defined source-to-target
Concept pairs in `constraints.yaml`.

## Inverse

The inverse Predicate is:

    governedBy
