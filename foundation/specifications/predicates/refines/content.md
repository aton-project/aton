# refines

## Meaning

An artifact provides a more detailed or specialized expression of another
engineering artifact while preserving its essential intent.

## Direction

The Predicate is directional.

The source provides a refinement of the target.

## Allowed Pairs

The Predicate is valid only for the explicitly defined source-to-target
Concept pairs in `constraints.yaml`.

## Inverse

The inverse Predicate is:

    refinedBy
