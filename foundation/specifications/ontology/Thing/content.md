# Thing

## Status

Draft

## Definition

A Thing is the most general conceptual category in the ATON ontology. It represents anything that can be meaningfully identified within the ATON knowledge model.

## Role in ATON

This ontology concept defines a semantic category used by the ATON
Foundation.

The concept SHALL be interpreted consistently across the ATON kernel,
Foundation artifacts and tooling.
