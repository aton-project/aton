# Artifact

## Status

Draft

## Definition

An Artifact is a persisted or represented engineering Entity within the ATON Foundation. An Artifact has identity, metadata, content and optional relations.

## Role in ATON

This ontology concept defines a semantic category used by the ATON
Foundation.

The concept SHALL be interpreted consistently across the ATON kernel,
Foundation artifacts and tooling.
