# Entity

## Status

Draft

## Definition

An Entity is the smallest independently identifiable unit of engineering knowledge in ATON. Every engineering object represented by the Foundation SHALL be an Entity.

## Role in ATON

This ontology concept defines a semantic category used by the ATON
Foundation.

The concept SHALL be interpreted consistently across the ATON kernel,
Foundation artifacts and tooling.
