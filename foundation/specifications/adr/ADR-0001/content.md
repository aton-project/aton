# ADR-0001 — Self-Hosting Foundation

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The Foundation defines the engineering knowledge used throughout the ATON
ecosystem.

To ensure consistency, completeness, and continuous validation, the Foundation
should describe itself using its own concepts and artifact model.

---

## Decision

The Foundation SHALL be authored using the Engineering Knowledge Model it
defines.

The Foundation SHALL describe its own concepts using Foundation artifacts.

The Foundation SHALL serve as the primary validation of its own architecture.

---

## Consequences

This decision provides:

- continuous validation of the Foundation
- immediate feedback for architectural changes
- dogfooding of the engineering knowledge model
- increased consistency across the ecosystem

---

## Rationale

Using the Foundation to describe itself demonstrates that the architecture is
practical and complete.

Every improvement to the Foundation immediately benefits both the Foundation
itself and every future ATON project.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### External Documentation

Rejected because maintaining separate documentation introduces duplication and
inconsistencies.

---

## Architectural Principle

> The Foundation is authored using the engineering knowledge model it defines.
