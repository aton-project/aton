# TERM-0001 Knowledge Unit

## Status

Draft

## Definition

A Knowledge Unit is an independently identifiable unit of engineering
knowledge represented within ATON.

A Knowledge Unit may describe a definition, decision, requirement,
specification, test, component, interface or other engineering concept.

## Role in ATON

The Knowledge Unit provides a semantic abstraction for engineering
knowledge that can be identified, persisted, related and processed by
the ATON system.

A Knowledge Unit is represented through the ATON Entity model.

## Relationship to Entity

Every Knowledge Unit SHALL be represented by an Entity.

The Entity model defines identity and persistence-independent
representation, while the Knowledge Unit describes the semantic role of
the represented engineering knowledge.
