# Entity

## Definition

An Entity is the smallest independently identifiable unit of engineering
knowledge in ATON.

An Entity represents an engineering object that can be identified, described,
related and processed within the ATON Engineering Knowledge Model.

## Role in ATON

Entity is a fundamental term of the ATON Engineering Knowledge Model.

The term refers to the conceptual unit of engineering knowledge and is
independent of its physical serialization or persistence mechanism.

Every engineering object represented by the ATON Foundation is represented
as an Entity.

## Relationship to the Entity Model

The normative Entity model is defined by ENTITY-0001 — Universal Entity Model.

The Glossary Entry defines the terminology used to refer to this concept. It
does not replace or redefine the normative Entity model.
