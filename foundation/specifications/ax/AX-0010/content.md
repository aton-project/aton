# AX-0010 — Trust & Transparency

## Purpose

This document defines how the ATON Experience establishes trust in engineering knowledge.

Users shall be able to understand the origin, evolution and reliability of every engineering artifact.

Trust shall be earned through transparency rather than assumption.

---

## Design Goals

The experience shall:

- expose provenance
- preserve history
- communicate authorship
- support verification
- encourage confidence

---

## Core Principles

Engineering knowledge shall be verifiable.

Every artifact shall provide sufficient information to establish trust.

Users shall never be required to rely on undocumented assumptions.

---

## Provenance

Users shall be able to identify:

- origin
- author
- creation time
- modification history
- contributing sources

---

## Version History

The evolution of engineering knowledge shall remain visible.

Users shall be able to understand what changed and why.

---

## Traceability

Traceability shall support trust.

Relationships between engineering artifacts shall remain visible and navigable.

---

## AI Transparency

AI-generated knowledge shall be clearly identifiable.

Whenever practical, supporting evidence and referenced artifacts shall be available.

---

## Consistency

Equivalent engineering knowledge shall always produce equivalent results.

The experience shall avoid ambiguity and hidden behavior.

---

## User Confidence

The interface shall communicate certainty without overstating confidence.

Unknown information shall remain explicitly unknown.

---

## Scalability

Trust and transparency shall remain independent of repository size, renderer implementation or AI model.
