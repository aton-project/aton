# AX-0006 — Interaction Model

## Purpose

This document defines how users interact with ATON.

Interactions shall support understanding, not merely manipulate user interface elements.

Every interaction shall feel predictable, consistent and efficient.

---

## Design Goals

Interactions shall:

- reduce cognitive effort
- provide immediate feedback
- remain consistent
- encourage exploration
- preserve user confidence

---

## Core Principles

Every interaction shall have a clear purpose.

Users shall never wonder what will happen next.

The system shall behave consistently in every context.

---

## Feedback

Every user action shall produce appropriate feedback.

Feedback shall be immediate whenever possible.

Long-running operations shall communicate progress.

---

## Discoverability

Available actions shall be easy to discover.

Hidden functionality shall be minimized.

Advanced functionality may be progressively disclosed.

---

## Predictability

Identical interactions shall always produce identical results.

Unexpected behavior shall be avoided.

---

## Reversibility

Whenever feasible, user actions shall be reversible.

Users shall feel safe exploring engineering knowledge.

---

## Efficiency

Common tasks shall require as few interactions as possible.

Frequent workflows shall minimize unnecessary user actions.

---

## Responsiveness

The interface shall acknowledge user input immediately.

Perceived responsiveness is part of the engineering experience.

---

## Scalability

Interaction patterns shall remain consistent regardless of repository size or renderer implementation.
