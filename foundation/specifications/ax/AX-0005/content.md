# AX-0005 — Visual Language

## Purpose

This document defines the visual language of the ATON Experience.

The visual language shall communicate engineering knowledge clearly,
consistently and efficiently.

Visual design exists to improve understanding.

---

## Design Goals

The visual language shall:

- emphasize knowledge
- reduce visual noise
- expose structure
- highlight relationships
- remain consistent

---

## Core Principles

Visual appearance shall never compete with engineering knowledge.

Every visual element shall communicate meaning.

Decoration without purpose shall be avoided.

---

## Typography

Typography shall maximize readability.

Visual hierarchy shall be created through structure rather than decoration.

Headings, body text and metadata shall remain visually consistent.

---

## Color

Color shall communicate meaning.

Color shall never be the only carrier of information.

Semantic colors shall remain consistent throughout the platform.

---

## Icons

Icons support recognition.

Icons shall complement text rather than replace it.

Every icon shall represent a single, well-defined concept.

---

## Spacing

Whitespace is part of the information architecture.

Spacing shall improve readability and reduce cognitive load.

---

## Visual Hierarchy

Important information shall naturally attract attention.

Hierarchy shall emerge through layout, spacing and typography.

---

## Consistency

Identical concepts shall always appear identically.

Users shall immediately recognize familiar engineering concepts.

---

## Scalability

The visual language shall remain effective from mobile devices to large engineering workstations.

It shall support repositories containing millions of artifacts without increasing visual complexity.
