# AX-0002 — Design Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the timeless design principles of the ATON Experience.

These principles guide every user interface, every component and every interaction.

---

## Principle 1 — Clarity First

Interfaces shall maximize understanding.

Visual complexity shall never exceed information value.

---

## Principle 2 — Knowledge First

Knowledge is the primary content.

The interface shall support knowledge, never compete with it.

---

## Principle 3 — Progressive Disclosure

Show only the information required for the current task.

Reveal additional detail on demand.

---

## Principle 4 — Consistency

Identical concepts shall always be represented identically.

Users shall never need to relearn familiar interactions.

---

## Principle 5 — Graph Native

Relationships are first-class citizens.

Connections shall be visible wherever they improve understanding.

---

## Principle 6 — Context Everywhere

Every artifact shall provide sufficient context to understand its purpose.

---

## Principle 7 — Accessibility

The experience shall remain usable for all users.

Accessibility is a design requirement, not an optional enhancement.

---

## Principle 8 — AI and Human Equality

Human users and AI agents consume the same engineering knowledge.

Presentation may differ.

Knowledge shall not.
