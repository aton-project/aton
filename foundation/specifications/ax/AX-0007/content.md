# AX-0007 — Knowledge Visualization

## Purpose

This document defines how engineering knowledge shall be visualized within ATON.

Visualizations shall improve understanding rather than decorate information.

Every visualization shall communicate structure, relationships and context.

---

## Design Goals

Knowledge visualization shall:

- reveal relationships
- expose hierarchy
- simplify complexity
- support exploration
- remain scalable

---

## Core Principles

Visualizations represent engineering knowledge.

They shall never distort or hide underlying information.

Users shall always be able to access the original artifact.

---

## Multiple Representations

Engineering knowledge may be presented as:

- documents
- lists
- tables
- trees
- graphs
- diagrams
- timelines

Different representations describe the same knowledge.

---

## Relationship Visualization

Relationships are first-class concepts.

Visualizations shall expose:

- parent relationships
- child relationships
- references
- dependencies
- traceability
- impact

---

## Graph Visualization

Knowledge graphs shall emphasize understanding over completeness.

Users shall be able to progressively expand graph complexity.

---

## Diagrams

Diagrams shall remain synchronized with the underlying engineering knowledge.

Generated diagrams shall never become independent sources of truth.

---

## Traceability

Traceability shall be directly visualized whenever it improves understanding.

Users shall immediately recognize upstream and downstream relationships.

---

## Consistency

The same engineering knowledge shall appear consistently across all visualizations.

Changing the representation shall not change the meaning.

---

## Scalability

Visualizations shall remain usable for repositories containing millions of artifacts.

Large knowledge graphs shall support progressive exploration.
