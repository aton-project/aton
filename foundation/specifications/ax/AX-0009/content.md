# AX-0009 — AI Experience

## Purpose

This document defines how AI agents participate in the ATON Experience.

AI is an equal consumer and producer of engineering knowledge.

The experience shall support seamless collaboration between humans and AI agents.

---

## Design Goals

The AI experience shall:

- improve understanding
- increase productivity
- preserve transparency
- encourage collaboration
- maintain user control

---

## Core Principles

AI shall augment engineering work.

AI shall never replace engineering responsibility.

Users remain accountable for engineering decisions.

---

## Shared Knowledge

Humans and AI agents shall access the same engineering knowledge.

No hidden knowledge sources shall exist.

Differences may exist only in presentation.

---

## Transparency

AI-generated content shall be identifiable.

Users shall understand the origin of generated knowledge.

Reasoning and supporting evidence shall be available whenever practical.

---

## Human Control

Users shall remain in control of engineering artifacts.

AI shall suggest rather than silently modify engineering knowledge.

User approval shall be required for persistent changes.

---

## Explainability

AI recommendations shall be explainable.

Whenever practical, AI shall reference the engineering knowledge supporting its conclusions.

---

## Collaboration

AI shall behave as an engineering assistant.

It may:

- answer questions
- summarize knowledge
- discover relationships
- identify inconsistencies
- suggest improvements

---

## Trust

Trust shall be earned through transparency and consistency.

AI confidence shall never replace engineering evidence.

---

## Scalability

The AI experience shall remain consistent regardless of repository size, AI model or renderer implementation.
