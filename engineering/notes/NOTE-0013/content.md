# NOTE-0013 — Engineering Relationship Semantics

## Context

The review of ADR-0005 identified that engineering relationships are required,
but their normative semantics are not yet defined.

---

## Problem Statement

Relationships are fundamental for traceability, navigation and impact
analysis throughout the Engineering Knowledge Model.

The Foundation currently defines that relationships shall exist but does not
define their semantics, characteristics or constraints.

---

## Questions

The engineering community should evaluate:

- Which relationship types exist?
- What is the semantic meaning of each relationship type?
- Which relationships are directional?
- Which relationships are symmetric?
- Which relationships are transitive?
- Which relationships are mandatory?
- Which relationships may be derived automatically?
- Which relationships require manual maintenance?
- How are relationship constraints defined?

---

## Expected Outcome

Define a normative engineering relationship model for the Engineering
Knowledge Model.
