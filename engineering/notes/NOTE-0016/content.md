# NOTE-0016 — Engineering Relationship Semantics

## Context

The review of ADR-0006 identified that relationships are fundamental elements
of the Engineering Knowledge Graph, but their normative semantics remain
undefined.

---

## Problem Statement

The Engineering Knowledge Graph depends on engineering relationships for
traceability, navigation and analysis.

The Foundation currently requires explicit relationships but does not define
their semantics.

---

## Questions

The engineering community should evaluate:

- What are the normative semantics of engineering relationships?
- Which relationship types exist?
- Which properties are common to all relationships?
- How are relationship constraints defined?
- Which relationships may be derived?
- Which relationships shall be maintained explicitly?

---

## Expected Outcome

Define the normative semantics of engineering relationships within the
Engineering Knowledge Model.
