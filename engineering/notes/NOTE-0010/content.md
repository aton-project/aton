# NOTE-0010 — Engineering Relation Semantics

## Context

The review of ADR-0004 identified that relationships between engineering
artifacts are required, but their semantics are not yet defined.

---

## Problem Statement

Engineering relationships are fundamental to traceability and knowledge
navigation.

The Engineering Knowledge Model currently does not define a normative set of
relation types or their semantics.

---

## Questions

The engineering community should evaluate:

- Which relation types exist?
- What is the semantic meaning of each relation type?
- Which relations are directional?
- Which relations are bidirectional?
- Which relations are transitive?
- Which relations are mandatory?
- Which relations may be derived automatically?
- Which relations are maintained manually?

---

## Expected Outcome

Define a normative relation model for the Engineering Knowledge Model,
including the semantics of all supported relation types.
