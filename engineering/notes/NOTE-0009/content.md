# NOTE-0009 — Engineering Version Semantics

## Context

The review of ADR-0004 identified that the semantics of versioning within the
Engineering Knowledge Model are currently undefined.

---

## Problem Statement

Versioning exists at multiple levels, including engineering entities,
artifacts, Git commits, baselines and releases.

The relationship between these concepts has not yet been defined.

---

## Questions

The engineering community should evaluate:

- What does versioning mean for an engineering entity?
- What does versioning mean for an engineering artifact?
- How are Git revisions related to engineering versions?
- How are baselines represented?
- How are releases represented?
- Which concepts are immutable?
- Which concepts evolve over time?

---

## Expected Outcome

Define a normative versioning model for the Engineering Knowledge Model.
