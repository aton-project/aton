# NOTE-0008 — Entity and Artifact Semantics

## Context

The review of ADR-0004 identified that the semantic distinction between
engineering entities and engineering artifacts is currently undefined.

---

## Problem Statement

The Engineering Knowledge Model distinguishes between entities and artifacts,
but their relationship has not yet been defined normatively.

---

## Questions

The engineering community should evaluate:

- What is an engineering entity?
- What is an engineering artifact?
- What is the relationship between entities and artifacts?
- Can one artifact contain multiple entities?
- Can one entity be represented by multiple artifacts?
- Which concept owns metadata?
- Which concept participates in traceability?

---

## Expected Outcome

Define the normative semantics of entities and artifacts within the Engineering
Knowledge Model.
