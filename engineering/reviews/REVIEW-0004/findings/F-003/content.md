# F-003 — Versioning semantics are ambiguous

## Observation

The ADR requires artifacts to be independently versionable.

## Problem

The architectural relationship between artifact versioning and engineering
knowledge versioning is not defined.

## Recommendation

Clarify the engineering semantics of versioning.

## Assessment

### Decision

Accepted

### Rationale

The engineering semantics of versioning affect multiple architectural concepts
and require a dedicated engineering discussion.
