# F-005 — Views should not be limited to documents

## Observation

The ADR describes documents as views over engineering knowledge.

## Problem

Engineering knowledge can also be presented through websites, APIs, IDEs, AI
assistants and other representations.

## Recommendation

Generalize the concept of views beyond documents.

## Assessment

### Decision

Accepted

### Rationale

Engineering knowledge should support multiple presentation formats beyond
documents.

### Classification

Category: architecture

### Disposition

Direct Implementation
