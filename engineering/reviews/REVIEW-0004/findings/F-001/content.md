# F-001 — Entity and Artifact are not distinguished

## Observation

ADR-0004 uses artifacts as the fundamental unit of engineering knowledge.

## Problem

The current Engineering Knowledge Model distinguishes between engineering
entities and their artifact representations.

## Recommendation

Clarify the architectural roles of entities and artifacts.

## Assessment

### Decision

Accepted

### Rationale

The Engineering Knowledge Model now distinguishes between entities and
artifacts. ADR-0004 should reflect this separation.

### Classification

Category: architecture

### Disposition

Direct Implementation
