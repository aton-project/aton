# F-002

## Observation

ADR-0001 does not distinguish between normative engineering knowledge and engineering process knowledge.

## Rationale

The current ATON architecture separates these concerns into the `foundation` and `engineering` domains. This separation improves the conceptual model, clarifies responsibilities and avoids mixing normative content with process artifacts.

## Assessment

Accepted.

ADR-0001 shall distinguish between the normative `foundation` domain and the engineering process domain.
