# F-004

## Observation

ADR-0001 extends the responsibility of a Review beyond the identification of findings by including engineering decisions and follow-up actions.

## Rationale

A Review shall only assess the reviewed artifact and document its findings. The evaluation of findings, including their acceptance or rejection and any resulting engineering activities, shall be performed outside the Review process. This separation establishes a clear distinction between review activities and engineering decision-making.

## Assessment

Accepted.

A Review shall only document findings. Engineering decisions and follow-up activities are performed outside the Review.
