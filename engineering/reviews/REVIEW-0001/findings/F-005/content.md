# F-005

## Observation

ADR-0001 models Findings as standalone artifacts.

## Rationale

A Finding only exists within the context of a Review. It has no independent meaning outside the Review and therefore shall be modeled as an embedded artifact of the corresponding Review.

## Assessment

Accepted.

The finding identifies a potential improvement to the Engineering Knowledge Model that extends beyond the reviewed artifact.

A NOTE shall be created to investigate the concept of embedded artifacts and to determine whether it should become part of the ATON architecture.
