# F-005 — Views should replace documents

## Observation

ADR-0006 contrasts the Engineering Knowledge Graph primarily with documents.

## Problem

The Foundation now distinguishes between engineering knowledge and its
different views, of which documents are only one example.

## Recommendation

Generalize the concept from documents to views.

## Assessment

### Decision

Accepted

### Rationale

The Foundation consistently distinguishes engineering knowledge from its
different views. ADR-0006 should use the generalized concept of views.
