# F-005 — Content definition is too narrow

## Observation

Engineering content is defined as technical knowledge.

## Problem

Engineering knowledge also includes architectural, organizational,
governance and process-related information.

## Recommendation

Use the broader term engineering knowledge.

## Assessment

### Decision

Accepted

### Rationale

The decision should consistently use the broader term engineering knowledge.

Category: editorial
