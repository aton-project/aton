# F-003 — Evolution of Engineering Knowledge

## Observation

ADR-0003 states that Git history is part of the engineering knowledge.

## Problem

The statement does not distinguish between engineering knowledge and its
historical evolution.

## Recommendation

Clarify the relationship between engineering knowledge and Git history.

## Assessment

### Decision

Accepted

### Rationale

The distinction between engineering knowledge and the evolution of engineering
knowledge should be expressed more precisely.

### Classification

Category: architecture

### Disposition

Direct Implementation
